Privacy Policy

QTA Consultants' QuickBooks integration accesses QuickBooks Online data only for companies that have authorized it through Intuit's consent screen, and only to perform bookkeeping work QTA has been engaged to provide.

  • Data accessed: accounting records (such as the chart of accounts, transactions, vendors and customers) needed for that work.
  • Storage: authorization tokens are stored encrypted on QTA's own computer. Records returned through the assistant are processed by Claude under the account’s applicable Anthropic terms. Local encrypted review batches retain snapshots and approval results. The GitHub Pages host receives the callback request URL, including a short-lived authorization code and company ID; the page promptly removes these from the address bar and forwards them only to the loopback receiver. No tokens or client secrets are published to GitHub. Hosting-provider request logs are outside this application’s control.
  • Changes: records are changed only after review and approval by QTA staff.
  • Disconnecting: a company can revoke access at any time from QuickBooks Online (Settings → Apps / connected apps) or by asking QTA.

Questions: contact your QTA Consultants representative.
